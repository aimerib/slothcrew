Datasets · Low-resource languages

Ticuna and Kichwa corpora

Data for two under-resourced languages of the Amazon. The Ticuna corpus combines parallel Ticuna–Spanish–Portuguese text, monolingual text, and a lexicon with the dictionary's tone notation, with the orthography tagged on every row. The Kichwa set consolidates and repairs three existing community uploads of a Napo Kichwa–Spanish dictionary into one documented, license-separated dataset.

Datasets · One idea each

Training-data experiments

Small datasets that each test one idea about how data shapes a model. Olivia-Sys rewrites a chat dataset without the hedging, sycophantic assistant voice. repremover-xl puts supervision at the exact turn where a model starts repeating itself.

03About

About.

I'm a senior software engineer with over seven years across DevOps and full-stack development, and these days most of it is pointed at AI. At ServicePros I work on bringing it into production, researching where it actually helps, and teaching people to use it well.

Before coming back, I spent two years at Poll Everywhere designing presentation streaming with Playwright, FFmpeg, and Go, modernizing code-signing infrastructure, and building geolocation features. In my first stint at ServicePros I led the modernization of legacy systems and rebuilt the CI/CD pipelines.

Outside work I train open models and build datasets, which I publish on Hugging Face, and I keep working on Nightrunner, a text-adventure engine in Rust. I also photograph people and products; that work lives at aimeribaddouh.com.
